FONTLOG for Stavan
==================

This file provides detailed information on the Stavan font software, as recommended by the SIL Open Font License.

Basic information
-----------------
Stavan is a handwriting font made from 675 pages of the author's own GoodNotes lecture notes, written with an Apple
Pencil. Letters were cut directly from the vector pen strokes stored in the notes (not scanned or traced), so they keep
the speed, slant and roughness of everyday writing. Every letter and digit has five versions from different pages,
which rotate through an OpenType `calt` feature. The rotation also counts spaces, so repeated words differ.

Weights: Regular (the GoodNotes fine pen) and Bold (a thicker pen over the same strokes).
Also: Stavan Mono (fixed width, for code) and Stavan Single Line (SVG font for pen plotters).
Coverage: printable ASCII, Latin-1 accented letters, curly quotes, en and em dashes, ellipsis.
