StavanMake your own

Draw each character once with a pen, finger or mouse, and you get a real font of your handwriting. Draw a few letters twice or three times and those versions take turns as you type, like mine do. Everything happens on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Draw

Small letters sit between the dashed line and the baseline. Tall letters and capitals reach the top line.

try it

Draw a few letters and they show up here. Type to see your handwriting.

Tips

Write the way you normally do, not the way you were taught. Draw each letter in one go and keep the strokes you would really make. Your drawings are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

The font installs like any other (double-click the file). Letters you skip fall back to the app's default font.

Part of Stavan, a font made from 675 pages of real notes. Source on GitHub.
